Ode to a Fox Who Is Definitely Not Jealous

Word came in from Hyderabad,
Of ma’am and “Bang” and fun they had.
The fox looked up, the fox said, “Cool.”
Then stared at nothing, like a fool.

She did Haiku, she cracked Mystery Room,
Binged Hostel Daze, then (oh, the doom)
Watched Kung Fu Panda. His film. His Po.
He’d have quoted every line, you know.

“I’m not jealous,” he declared,
To no one, since nobody cared.
He said it twice, he said it thrice,
By seven times, it sounded nice.

His fever climbed one point, then two.
“It’s just the weather,” he said. “Achoo.”
Jignesh checked his chart and sighed:
“Acute Bang-itis, left untried.”

He put the Barney suit back on,
Said, “I’ll be awesome. Feelings, be gone.”
But awesome only lasts so long
When someone else is in the song.

So here’s the fox’s humble plea:
No flirting till December? Agreed. (Mostly.)
But if a line slips out by mistake,
Don’t go red, ma’am. For Po’s sake.

P.S. Dumb fox has requested this poem be clearly labelled fiction. HR has labelled it “Evidence.”EVIDENCE
