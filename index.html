Kunjum-Kunjum Post

A newsletter with a readership of one

Kunjum-Kunjum

A stretch of silence, soft and wide,
two continents, a sky between.
You asked for room to heal inside,
so I don't knock; I just stay seen.

But some nights a jam session begins,
Your queue, my queue, then half of each
No typing dots, no how've you beens,
just music taking us where we can't reach.

And each morning, like a sign
Hung above a lady's door,
In your notes I see a few lines,
Just one song is enough to lure.

Is it tease, or is it ache?
Is it "wait," or is it "come"?
Either way, the chords still shake
something only you can strum.

For I know the road you tread,
Still is warm from other feet.
Let the old words all be said,
Ere the new ones find their beat.
Love that hurries, love that pleads,
Builds on sand and fades by day.
I will plant my patient seeds
In the silence, far away.

But now December's on its way:
a crowded gate, and you, and then
the run you promised me that day.
One kiss, and I am home again.

And who will let the L-word slip?
Guard it, darling, till the kiss.
And I might lose it, lip to lip:
Worth every penny, losing this.

We'd never say it, you and I;
it's our rule, and I obey.
Still, kunjum-kunjum, by the by,
I miss you more than I can say.
