Confession Page

Due to a massive data leak (and a mental block), we regret to inform you that there's no poem this time around. So, so... to make up for it, here's a completely made-up story, pulled straight out of my arse, about a girl.

Manasi, you know what? I think I'm in like with her. She's something else, honestly one of the most complete women I've ever met.

She aces everything she touches: studies, dance, swimming, and, kunjum-kunjum, the piano. She's a books-and-romcoms nerd with annoyingly good taste in music, my very own RJ, a future dermatologist, and on top of all that, she just glows. I mean, what the actual fuxk? She's taking perfection to its absolute limit, and I'm standing here, simply awestruck. Uff, nazar na lage kisi ki.

In the end, I just want to tell her: of all the places I could be, I want to be here with you.

So, Manasi... what should I do about her?
